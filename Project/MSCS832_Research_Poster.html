Abstract

Foundation of Smart Urban Living

Rapid urbanization and IoT proliferation drive cities towards data-driven, connected, and sustainable smart infrastructures. Smart networks must interconnect billions of heterogeneous devices while meeting stringent QoS and QoE requirements, remaining energy-efficient, secure, and scalable.

Key Research Focus:

  • Next-generation networks (5G/6G)
  • Network slicing and edge computing
  • AI-driven network management
  • Mathematical optimization models
  • Practical use cases in transportation, energy, healthcare
Keywords: Smart city, IoT, 5G, 6G, edge computing, network slicing, QoS, QoE, AI, security

Introduction

The Rise of Smart Cities and Networks

"A smart network is a programmable, adaptive, and intelligent infrastructure designed for smart city ecosystems."

Five Core Requirements:

  • Device Support: Massive heterogeneous IoT devices
  • Reliability: Low latency and high reliability for critical services
  • Efficiency: Optimized energy consumption
  • Security: Robust privacy and trust mechanisms
  • Intelligence: Real-time AI and data analytics adaptation

Key Technologies: 5G/6G, SDN, NFV, edge/fog computing, machine learning

Background & Technology

Smart City Applications:

  • Transportation: Traffic lights, connected vehicles
  • Energy: Smart meters, demand-response control
  • Healthcare: Remote monitoring, telemedicine
  • Buildings: HVAC, smart lighting, air quality
  • Governance: E-government, digital identity

5G/6G Capabilities:

  • eMBB: Enhanced Mobile Broadband
  • URLLC: Ultra-reliable low-latency communications
  • mMTC: Massive machine-type communications
  • Network Slicing: Logical network optimization
  • 6G: Integrated sensing, AI-native management
